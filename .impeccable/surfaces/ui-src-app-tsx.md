## Direction contract

THESIS: The response clock as a laboratory counter: every count and countdown is a lit numeral in glass, so the channel's record reads as a measurement nobody typed. Refuses the category's white-and-navy dashboard of stat cards.

OWN-WORLD: Blackened, slightly warm steel chassis; gunmetal panels with engraved plate edges and corner screws; plasma orange only where a value is live; steel-grey condensed display caps; unlit numerals faintly visible behind the lit one; status lamps with words, never colour alone; no neon blues or purples.

STORY: A verifier sees at once that Acme answers on time, reads each case's row, understands what stays sealed. An employee sees the panel switch "File a report", trusts what Lace will show, files, reads replies. The officer works the desk with the same instruments.
